## 1️⃣ What a Temple really is  
- **Spiritual hub** – It's a place where people go to connect with the divine, pray, do puja, and seek blessings.  
- **Cultural centre** – Festivals, music, dance, art‑forms, and community gatherings all happen here.  
- **Architectural marvel** – Every temple is a story in stone, with symbolism baked into every pillar, dome, and corridor.
