Q+A Katha - Family 🧠

Question: Who played Deputy Marshal Samuel Gerard in the 1993 film "The Fugitive"?

Answer: Tommy Lee Jones - Entertainment: Film. Itha kelvi Family pathi - Answer Tommy Lee Jones. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Family pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Family la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Family oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
Q+A Katha - Friendship 🧠

Question: Which of the following landlocked countries is entirely contained within another country?

Answer: Lesotho - Geography. Itha kelvi Friendship pathi - Answer Lesotho. Friendship pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Friendship pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Friendship oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Friendship ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Friendship la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
